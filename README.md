## Top Hospital Command Center Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Hospital Operations Command Centers, Patient Flow, Capacity Management, Care Coordination & Operational Intelligence*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/Hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Hospital Command Centers**. These systems integrate real-time hospital data to provide centralized visibility and decision support across patient flow, bed capacity, emergency departments, operating rooms, staffing, transfers, discharge planning, care coordination, and operational performance.

**Examples** include GE HealthCare Command Center, TeleTracking, Care.ai, Qventus, LeanTaaS, Central Logic, Philips Command Center, Palantir Foundry for Health, Hospital IQ, and Artisight.

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with active open-source hospital operations, patient-flow, bed-management, healthcare analytics, hospital-information-system, interoperability, workflow, and command-center projects — ideal for hospitals, researchers, developers, health systems, and organizations building self-hosted operational intelligence platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[PatientFlow](https://github.com/UCL-CORU/patientflow)**  
  Open-source Python package for predicting short-term hospital bed demand from real-time or near-real-time patient information. It is explicitly designed to support bed managers and short-term operational planning. 

- **[Hospital Operations Analytics](https://github.com/Denis0242/Hospital-Operations-Analytics)**  
  Open-source hospital operations analytics project providing dashboards for patient flow, capacity utilization, readmissions, length of stay, department performance, and operational bottlenecks. 

- **[ClinicalOS](https://github.com/DrARTBuilds/ClinicalOS)**  
  Open-source AI-native hospital operations platform designed as an operational intelligence layer above existing clinical systems, with operations dashboards, patient intake, doctor routing, bottleneck analytics, billing workflows, and decision intelligence. 

- **[MedCore](https://github.com/Globussoft-Technologies/medcore)**  
  Open-source hospital information system covering clinical, operational, and financial workflows including admissions, emergency, bed management, live ER boards, pharmacy, laboratory, surgery, billing, and patient engagement. 

- **[BedFlow AI](https://github.com/draculess99/BedFlow_AI)**  
  Open-source AI-driven hospital bed-management project designed to predict bed availability and recommend patient placement to reduce bottlenecks and waiting times.

- **[BedFlow](https://github.com/NunoSid/BedFlow)**  
  Open-source digital health project focused on clinical handover and bed-management workflows, translating hospital operational processes into structured and auditable workflows.

- **[Hospital Bed Management System](https://github.com/Ansarimajid/Hospital-Management-System)**  
  Open-source hospital bed-management implementation focused on monitoring availability and allocating beds within hospital facilities.

- **[OpenMRS](https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-core)**  
  Major open-source medical-record platform that can provide clinical and patient data foundations for hospital operational dashboards, analytics, and command-center integrations.

- **[OpenEMR](https://github.com/openemr/openemr)**  
  Open-source electronic health records and medical practice management platform that can act as a source system for operational analytics and hospital workflow integrations.

- **[GNU Health](https://github.com/gnuhealth/health)**  
  Open-source health information system covering clinical, hospital, patient, and administrative workflows that can contribute data to a broader operational command-center architecture.

- **[Bahmni](https://github.com/Bahmni/bahmni)**  
  Open-source hospital information system built around OpenMRS and related technologies, providing clinical, laboratory, pharmacy, and hospital-management workflows.

- **[OpenELIS Global](https://github.com/openelisglobal/openelisglobal-core)**  
  Open-source laboratory information system that can provide laboratory workflow and turnaround-time data for hospital operational dashboards.

- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**  
  Open-source supply-chain and inventory-management system for healthcare organizations, useful for integrating medical-supply visibility into broader hospital operations command centers.

- **[DHIS2](https://github.com/dhis2/dhis2-core)**  
  Open-source health information and data platform supporting analytics, dashboards, reporting, and health-system data aggregation.

- **[FHIR / HAPI FHIR](https://github.com/hapifhir/hapi-fhir)**  
  Open-source FHIR implementation providing interoperability infrastructure for connecting EHR, laboratory, patient-flow, and operational systems to a centralized command-center data layer.

- **[Smile CDR](https://github.com/smilecdr)**  
  FHIR-based healthcare interoperability technology that can be incorporated into healthcare data-integration architectures.

- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**  
  Open-source data visualization and business-intelligence platform suitable for building hospital operations dashboards, KPI views, capacity monitoring, and executive command-center analytics.

- **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)**  
  Open-source observability and visualization platform that can provide real-time hospital operational dashboards when connected to appropriate healthcare data sources.

- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  
  Open-source business-intelligence platform useful for hospital KPI reporting, operational analytics, and self-service command-center dashboards.

- **[Apache Airflow](https://github.com/apache/airflow)**  
  Open-source workflow orchestration platform suitable for building data pipelines that ingest and transform EHR, ADT, bed, staffing, laboratory, and operational data.

- **[Dagster](https://github.com/dagster-io/dagster)**  
  Open-source data orchestration platform useful for building reliable hospital data pipelines and operational analytics infrastructure.

- **[Prefect](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/prefect)**  
  Open-source workflow orchestration framework that can coordinate healthcare data pipelines feeding hospital command-center analytics.

- **[Kafka](https://github.com/apache/kafka)**  
  Open-source event-streaming platform suitable for real-time ingestion of hospital events such as admissions, discharges, transfers, bed changes, lab events, and operational alerts.

- **[Apache Flink](https://github.com/apache/flink)**  
  Open-source stream-processing engine that can support real-time hospital-event processing and operational analytics.

- **[Apache Spark](https://github.com/apache/spark)**  
  Open-source distributed analytics engine useful for large-scale hospital data processing, forecasting, and operational intelligence.

- **[PostgreSQL](https://github.com/postgres/postgres)**  
  Open-source relational database suitable for storing normalized operational, patient-flow, capacity, and command-center data.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **PatientFlow** for short-term bed-demand forecasting and patient-flow analytics. Its design specifically targets operational planning by bed managers rather than individual clinical decision-making. 
- **ClinicalOS** for experimenting with an AI-native hospital operations layer and real-time command-center architecture.
- **MedCore** for an integrated open-source hospital information system containing operational data such as beds, admissions, emergency workflows, laboratory turnaround times, and surgery.
- **BedFlow AI** for predictive bed-availability and patient-placement experimentation.
- **Hospital Operations Analytics** for executive KPI dashboards, capacity strain monitoring, patient flow, and operational bottleneck analysis.
- **OpenMRS** for clinical data infrastructure that can feed operational intelligence applications.
- **Bahmni** for an integrated open-source hospital information system.
- **OpenELIS Global** for laboratory operational data.
- **OpenBoxes** for hospital supply-chain visibility.
- **HAPI FHIR** for healthcare interoperability and standardized data exchange.
- **Apache Superset** and **Grafana** for command-center visualization.
- **Kafka** and **Flink** for real-time hospital event streaming.
- **Airflow**, **Dagster**, and **Prefect** for healthcare data orchestration.
- **PostgreSQL** for a foundational operational data store.

**Frameworks for building custom hospital command centers**: Combine **HAPI FHIR**, **OpenMRS**, **Bahmni**, **OpenEMR**, **Kafka**, **Flink**, **PostgreSQL**, **Airflow/Dagster**, **Grafana**, **Apache Superset**, **Metabase**, and **PatientFlow** to build an open operational-intelligence layer over existing hospital systems.

A modern open-source hospital command-center architecture can therefore be divided into:

1. **Clinical/EHR Data** — OpenMRS, OpenEMR, Bahmni
2. **Hospital Information System** — MedCore, Bahmni, GNU Health
3. **Interoperability** — HAPI FHIR, FHIR, OpenHIM
4. **ADT / Patient Flow** — PatientFlow, BedFlow, custom event streams
5. **Bed Management** — BedFlow AI, hospital bed-management projects
6. **Laboratory Operations** — OpenELIS Global
7. **Supply Chain** — OpenBoxes
8. **Event Streaming** — Kafka, Apache Flink
9. **Data Engineering** — Airflow, Dagster, Prefect, Spark
10. **Operational Database** — PostgreSQL
11. **Analytics** — Python, R, DuckDB
12. **Dashboards** — Grafana, Apache Superset, Metabase
13. **AI/Forecasting** — PatientFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost
14. **AI Agents & Workflow Automation** — LangGraph, n8n, custom healthcare agents
15. **Identity & Access** — Keycloak, Authentik
16. **Observability** — Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow existing format).
3. Include: name, link, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it's SaaS or open-source.
4. Prioritize actively maintained projects with meaningful hospital operations, patient-flow, capacity-management, bed-management, interoperability, healthcare analytics, or command-center functionality.
5. Verify the project's current license before describing it as open source.
6. Submit a PR with a short explanation.

Star the repo if you find it useful!

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated list** — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Commercial hospital command-center platforms and open-source projects have substantially different feature sets, deployment models, integrations, scalability, support models, and healthcare-specific functionality.
- Some projects listed here are broader EHR, HIS, interoperability, analytics, workflow, or data-platform projects rather than dedicated hospital command-center products.
- GitHub contains many hospital-management and patient-flow projects that may be experimental, educational, incomplete, or inactive; repository inclusion does not imply production readiness.
- Open-source licensing varies between projects; always verify the current license and any enterprise/community-edition restrictions before deployment.
- Healthcare command-center systems often process highly sensitive patient and operational data. Production deployments require appropriate security, privacy, access control, audit logging, availability, backup, interoperability, and regulatory controls.
- Predictive analytics and AI systems should support, rather than independently replace, appropriate clinical and operational judgment.
- Synthetic or anonymized datasets used in open-source projects should not be assumed to represent real-world hospital performance.
